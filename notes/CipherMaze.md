# Background

CipherMaze is the course project for COMP2113.

# Why this theme?

By the time our team was deciding the theme of our game, COMP1110 had just gone through the topic "Cybersecurity & Cryptography".  
In that lecture, the lecturer introduced Caesae cipher and substitution cipher to us. He also demonstrated once how to make use of frequency analysis to crack substitution cipher.
