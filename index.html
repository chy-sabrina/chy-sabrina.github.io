Cipher Maze

Mar 2026 – May 2026

COMP2113 Course Project; Score: 19.9/20

A text-based cryptography puzzle game where players navigate through a map filled with various rooms. The objective is to successfully navigate through the map by solving puzzles and reaching the end goal.

There are two types of rooms: chest room and enemy room. In chest rooms, players will encounter puzzles that require them to decrypt the ciphertext to get weapons. In enemy rooms, players will face enemies that require them to use the weapons to defeat. If players encounter enemies without weapon, a corresponding amount of HP will be deducted.

Features Caesar, numeric, and substitution ciphers combined with fog-of-war exploration and combat mechanics.

Designed the game's theme, implemented substitution cipher gameplay, developed tutorial and hint systems, and led testing and debugging.
